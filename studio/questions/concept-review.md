# Independent concept review

Claude has already evaluated Clean Slate. Before you read anything Claude concluded, give your own independent assessment, using only the project context. The owner will compare both views.

The defining product is putting a relationship's digital history out of sight without surrendering the possibility of getting it back. Go Back may be a primary reason to buy the $100 cleanup. The ex-held password is deliberate and stays; you may raise risks about it, but do not propose removing it.

Please evaluate, briefly and concretely:

1. The emotional value proposition, and whether it can justify $100 one time.
2. A manual-first product versus an AI-assisted product for the first release.
3. How Go Back influences the first purchase, even if restoration is never used.
4. Revenue from repeated cleanup and restoration cycles: realistic or wishful?
5. Onboarding and experience ideas that set Clean Slate apart from cheap photo-cleaning apps.
6. Which parts are technically feasible now and which remain uncertain.

End with the single cheapest test that would tell the owner whether to build this, and the one assumption you think is most likely wrong. Separate evidence from opinion, and distinguish real obstacles from choices that are merely unconventional.
